stale year

warning stale-year Freshness

The same past year in the headings of a whole section: "Best plumbers in Austin 2024", "…in Boston 2024", "…in Dallas 2024" in 2026 — the heading template nobody updated in January. One page with a year in its title says nothing on its own ("2019 Honda Civic", "The Batman (2022) review", "Paris Olympics 2024" are about that year), so the finding needs five or more pages and at least a third of the section sharing the year. Ranges that reach the current year ("2025–2026", "2025/26"), starting points ("since 2015") and pages dated in that same year are not counted. One finding per section.

What indexgap reports

The finding reads:

warning  stale-year  … of … pages of the /… section have … in the heading, and it is …: the heading template was not updated. For example: …

How to fix it

Drop the year from the heading template or fill in the current one from the build's data. If the page is that year's history, give it a publication date.

How it behaves at scale

Collapsed when it fires template-wide. If it appears on 90% or more of the pages — or of one language version — of a site with at least 20 pages, it is a property of the template and is reported once, with the count, instead of once per page.

Finding it

pipx install indexgap
cd your-project
indexgap init
indexgap check

Then indexgap brief turns every finding on this page into a work order in indexgap-briefs/, with the thresholds the result has to satisfy and the command to check it.
